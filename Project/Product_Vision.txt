For blind and visually impaired individuals who face challenges in navigating spaces and locating objects independently, the AR Accessibility Guide is a mobile and wearable augmented reality (AR) application that provides real-time audio and haptic feedback to help users identify obstacles, directions, and objects in their environment. Unlike traditional tools such as canes or static navigation applications, our product offers an immersive, real-time AR experience that empowers users to move safely, efficiently, and independently.
